The issues with no Part# are not actually marked on the cover as being part of the storyline. However, all of these issues have their reasons for being included in the list, as indicated in the notes that follow:

- Nightwing #67 - Continues directly from the mid-scene ending of Nightwing #66. 

- Batman: Gotham Knights #27 - No facts are shed involving the case, but the Murderer/Fugitive storyline is the central hook of the issue. 

- Batman #602 - Part 2 of a 2-part arch that began in Batman #601. 

- Detective Comics #768 - Part 1 of a 3-part arch that continues through Detective Comics #769 and #770. 

- Batman: Gotham Knights #29 - Part 2 of a 2-part arch that began in Batman: Gotham Knights #28. 

- Azrael: Agent of the Bat #92 - Continues directly from the mid-scene ending of Azrael: Agent of the Bat #91. 

- Batman #604 - It has Batman in it, duh, and it takes place between crossover issues, placing it in this storyline. The end of this issue plays into Batman's state of mind in Batman #605, the final issue of the crossover.
